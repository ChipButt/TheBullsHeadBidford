36 High Street · Our village story

Two centuries onBidford High Street

We're lucky to be part of a village with so much history. Bidford grew up around a crossing of the River Avon, where people were travelling long before our pub opened its doors. The old stone bridge has been part of village life for centuries, and we love being just a short walk from it.

Back in the nineteenth century, our High Street was full of village shops and trades. There were seven pubs and inns in Bidford in 1861, and The Bulls Head was one of them. It's lovely to think of all the conversations that have happened here as the village has changed around us.

People have been coming to The Bulls Head for a pint and a catch-up for more than 200 years. We're proud to carry that tradition on at 36 High Street, and our hope is simple: to make this somewhere friends, neighbours and visitors always feel welcome.

ABOUT US

Meet the landlordsAnouska & Simon

We took over The Bulls Head in August 2025 with a simple vision: to bring the heart and community spirit back into our beautiful traditional village pub.

Since then, we’ve been the faces of the pub and working hard behind the scenes. The support we’ve received has been humbling, and seeing the community come together for our charity events has made us especially proud.

We’re delighted to have raised £700 for “I’m Homeless Get Me Out of Here”, and we’re proud to support the British Legion Poppy Appeal every year.

Our regular acoustic evenings are becoming a favourite with musicians and customers alike, whether they’re playing or joining in with a singalong.

The journey hasn’t always been easy, but we’re getting there. We hope to be serving food soon.

We’re proud to be the publicans of The Bulls Head, and we look forward to welcoming you.
